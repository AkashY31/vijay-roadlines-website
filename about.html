About Vijay Roadlines

Three years of showing up for the load

We're guided by three things: cost, quality of service, and getting there on time. That's it — practiced consistently by a team that has spent years doing nothing but FTL and PTL transportation.

In just over three years we've become one of the transportation names companies in the NCR region actually rely on — not the cheapest quote, but the one that shows up.

Alongside our regular fleet we keep 10–15 vehicles in reserve, ready to move the moment a breakdown, roadblock, or traffic snarl threatens a delivery window. Every vehicle on the road carries GPS tracking, a first-aid box, FASTag, and a toolbox — because a shipment shouldn't stop for something we could have planned for.
